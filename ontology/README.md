# Ontology

Versioned data, not code: the kinds of fact the system keeps and the rule tables code applies to them. Each file is read and checked by the code that uses it, and a file's `version` is raised when a value changes. Personal Intelligence's fact types, with their fields and checks, are code in `packages/beliefs/src/ontology.ts`.

- `kinds.json`: the one kind registry. For each kind of fact about the owner and their people: its fact type and belief kind, the subjects it may be about, its fields with its key, what the extraction prompts say it means, where the rest goes, and synthetic good and bad examples. Structure tests hold the TypeScript tables to it, and `node tools/kinds/generate.ts` writes `packages/contracts/src/personal/kinds.generated.ts` from it.
- `belief-kinds.yaml`: the belief kinds, and which view owns each.
- `relations.json`: the rules for relations between facts (the same fact, one superseding another, a contradiction): the thresholds that switch automatic relations on and off, the order in which evidence wins and its margins, and the relation step's and the nightly pass's budgets. Read by `relation-rules.ts` in core.
- `warmth.json`: the warmth tier's constants: the half-lives of recent and long-standing contact, the weights of a meeting, a one-to-one meeting and an answered thread, the tier sizes and floors, and the path weight of each tier. Read by `warmth.ts` in core.
- `areas.json`: the areas a read about a person can be narrowed to (self, work, needs, ties, rules, activity), each as the belief kinds it keeps. An area narrows a read and never unlocks one.
- `goals.json`: a goal's review window in days, for a goal the owner set and for one they stated; past it, a goal with no new support is stale.
- `autonomy.json`: Personal Intelligence's autonomy windows: the hours ahead a meeting raises a prep task (`meeting_soon`), the hours ahead an open commitment's due time raises a follow-up task (`commitment_due`), the days unanswered and the most days old a mail raises a reply task (`reply_owed`), whether a dated thing with nobody on it raises a task (`deadlineSoonOn`, off), and the days a deferred card waits before it expires by the policy. Provisional rule data; its `version` raised when a value changes.
- `second-degree.json`: Personal Intelligence's second degree and frontier: the named people a label may gain a day; the path weights of an acquaintance hop, an offer's hop by its offer, and a reported or researched hop, each 0 to 1, how many days an offer counts and the walk's hop bound, at most 4; the hours ahead a meeting with a namer counts (`namedMeetingHours`); the second-degree people a pack shows per attendee and the map per node; the non-goal nodes explored per label a day and the days an explored node rests; and `value`, the weights a frontier node's value is scored by. Loaded and checked by core's `secondDegreeRules()`, which refuses a hop weight outside 0 to 1. Provisional rule data; its `version` raised when a value changes.
- `questions.json`: Personal Intelligence's owner questions: the pairs one agent's task or the owner's one card holds, the offers before an unanswered pair reaches the owner, and how many agreeing answers propose a rule.
- `activity-categories.json`: Personal Intelligence's activity categories: a versioned table of rows, each a bundle id or a host (matching its subdomains, with an optional URL path prefix) and one category of nine. Code applies it to Lens's time blocks when they are read (`pi lens time`); nothing it gives is stored. Product names only; extended by a commit, its `version` raised when a row changes.
- `contact-quality.json`: Personal Intelligence's contact quality rules: the most words an organization name has, the characters and the first and last words that make a name or a title a headline's, the legal suffixes a comma may stand before, the messaging and texting providers' domains that name no organization, and the local parts of a function's mailbox. Code applies them to a contact's organizations and titles before they become claims (`contact-claims@3`, `contact-import@5`). Provisional rule data; its `version` raised when an entry changes.
- `outcomes.json`: Personal Intelligence's outcome rules: each opportunity kind's default expected outcome and horizon (or none), the horizon of a stated expectation that gives none, and the 90-day grace period a closed work's watches stay compared for. Provisional rule data.
- `public-suffix-list.dat`: the Public Suffix List (https://publicsuffix.org/list/public_suffix_list.dat, MPL 2.0), pinned and refreshed by hand, its version and fetch date in its header, comments stripped. Personal Intelligence folds an organization's domain to its registrable domain by it.
